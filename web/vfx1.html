VFX1 head tracking

starting…
  • Tap Start first so the browser allows sound, then Enter VR once DOS is running.
  • Mouse head tracking (tracking=mouse): in Descent set Options → Controls to use the mouse, or head tracking has nothing to drive.
  • Controllers in VR, everything on the right hand: trigger fires, pressing the stick fires secondary, grip accelerates, stick up/down pitches (and moves through menus), stick left/right turns the ship, A is Enter (not during native stereo flight), B is Escape. Left hand, optional: trigger fires secondary, stick forward accelerates, stick back reverses, stick left/right turns, X recentres your head. With native tracking the right controller is also the CyberPuck: its tilt from where you held it at the last recentre, and trigger, grip and A as the puck's buttons. With puckmouse=on it is also the mouse, as Forte's VRMOUSE made it: tilt to fly, trigger and grip to fire.
  • For puckmouse=on, in Descent: Options, Controls, set the control method to Mouse, then customise the mouse: Pitch and Turn to none (your head does those), Throttle to mouse Y, Slide left/right or Bank to mouse X.
